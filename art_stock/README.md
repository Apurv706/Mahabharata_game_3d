# Paintings waiting for their story

Paintings the artist has delivered for work that is not built yet. They are kept here, outside `art/`, so that the
published game does not carry files it never shows (each published part may hold at most 512 files).

When a story line is built, move its paintings into `art/` and name them in the scene; `tests/content.js` then checks
that each part carries what it shows.
