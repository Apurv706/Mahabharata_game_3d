# Repaint brief: which paintings to redo from the artist's sheets

*15 November 2026. Written after the decision that the artist's sheets are the true look. The same list with every painting beside its sheet is on the page [Kurukshetra Repaint List](https://claude.ai/artifact/FDy7wCE4exGo6UwDK93qp7).*

## In short

Eighteen portraits and nine story paintings show a hero who contradicts his own sheet. Those are the ones to repaint.

| What | How many |
|---|---|
| Portraits to repaint (the head or the dress is another man's) | 13 |
| Portraits to touch up (close, a detail or two missing) | 5 |
| Story paintings to repaint the people in | 9 paintings, 14 picture files |
| Film clips to remake | 3 |
| Smaller touch-ups, only if every picture should agree | 11 scenes, 16 files |
| Pictures that stay as they are | 436 of 484 |

The portraits matter most. A portrait stands beside what a person says and on every bond, order and challenge card, so it is the face the player learns.

## Rules for every repaint

**Keep the picture. Change the person.** The paintings are dark, warm and lamp-lit, painted like oils. The sheets are flat and bright, drawn as a reference. A repaint keeps the painting's own style, light, pose and background, and takes from the sheet only who the person is: headgear, hair, beard, skin, jewellery, dress and its colours. (This is Claude's reading of the decision. If the paintings should move toward the brighter style of the sheets, far more than this list would need repainting.)

- **Portraits:** upright, 3 wide to 4 high, 960 by 1280 or larger. Head and shoulders to the chest. The game shows the face inside a small circle (the top three quarters of the picture, full width), so the whole head with its crown must sit in the upper two thirds, in the middle, with a little air above the crown.
- **Story paintings:** 16 wide to 9 high, 2560 by 1440 or larger. Keep the main people inside the middle band of the height, because the long banner version (3 to 1) is cut from the same picture.
- **Film clips:** 10 seconds, 1280 by 720, opening on the new painting. Until a new clip arrives the old one is switched off for that scene, so the old dress never plays over the new painting.
- **Names:** use the file names given here. Any picture format will do.

## The portraits

One portrait serves a person for the whole game, so each follows one sheet: the court dress for those known in peace, the war dress for those met in the war. Done in batches, the first should be Arjuna, Bhima, Yudhishthira and Karna, who are in the story from its first acts to the war.

## What stays, and why

- **Places with no people:** the 50 stage backdrops, the 21 fight backdrops, the homes, the map and the emblems.
- **Crowds, hands and distant figures:** the halls, the armies, the endings, the tokens of vows and paths. Nobody in them can be told apart.
- **People with no sheet:** the player's own family and friends, the common folk, Bhagadatta on his elephant.
- **Paintings that already agree with the sheets:** Drona, Kripa and old Mauni with their students (teacher_drona, teacher_kripa, teacher_mauni); Kripa in the corridor (scene_mentor_kripa); Drona on the fifteenth day (war_day15); Ekalavya, Drona and the princes (scene_ekalavya); Gandhari on the field (scene_women_field); Vidura's banner (scene_mentor_vidura).
- **The bed of arrows** (`scene_arrows`): Bhishma lies in dark armour where his sheet gives silver, but he is small, the kings round him are smaller, and the scene has a film clip. Left alone.
- **Paintings that show the player:** the lessons, the childhood memories and the endings show a man or a boy, whoever the player chose to be. That is a separate question from the sheets and is left alone here.

## Faces still missing

New painting, not repainting, and nothing waits on it. These people have a sheet and a bond with the player in the story, but no portrait at all: Krishna, Draupadi, Dushasana, Shakuni, Balarama and Prince Uttara. Shalya, Jayadratha, Shikhandi, Nakula and Sahadeva have sheets too and stand in the war. A portrait for each, painted from the sheet in the same lamp-lit style, would give every named person a face. (Each new portrait also needs its name added to `PORTRAITS` in `js/ui/dom.js`.)

## Making do without a painter

Asked the same day: can the game do without the repaintings? It can. Nothing breaks; what is lost is the match between a painted hero and the 3D hero built from his sheet.

- **Portraits, with no painter:** the 18 can be cut from the sheets themselves. The head and shoulders are lifted off the paper, the sheet's title is painted out, and the figure is set on a dark, lamp-lit ground and graded warm. A test of eight (Arjuna, Bhima, Yudhishthira, Karna, Kripa, Bhishma, Satyaki, Abhimanyu) was made and shown to the owner. It holds up at the size the game shows a face (a circle of 22 to 74 points).
- **What that costs:** the cut portraits are drawn, not painted, so they differ in style from the 13 painted portraits that stay, most of all where a memory shows a portrait large in a gold frame. Every court sheet is bare-chested. And it puts cuts of the sheets into `art/`, which the owner has so far kept out of the repo.
- **Story paintings:** left as they are. They read as illustration, and the hero's dress in nine of them will differ from his 3D self.
- **Later, another way:** portraits photographed from the 3D heroes once they wear their sheet dress.
- **State:** a test only. Nothing is in the game. The reference pictures for a real repaint were also handed over as a zip, one folder per job.

## How this was checked

Every one of the 484 picture files in `art/` was looked at, at least as a thumbnail, the banner, wide and tall versions separately, because the banner and the wide painting of one scene are often different pictures (Shikhandi wears bronze in one and silver in the other). One frame of each of the 26 film clips was looked at as well. Each picture that shows a named person at a size where dress and face can be read was then set beside that person's sheet.

## When the new pictures arrive

- Portraits go to `art/p_<name>.webp` at 480 by 640.
- Story paintings keep their present names and sizes (listed above). Where a scene has a banner and a wide version, both are made from the one new picture.
- A repainted scene with a film clip loses its name from `VIDEOS` in `js/ui/dom.js` until the new clip is in `video/`. `war_wheel_open` is the first frame of the wheel clip and is remade with it.
- Then both parts of the game are published again and the costume notes updated.
