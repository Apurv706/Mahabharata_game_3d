# The 3D figures beside the artist's sheets (16 November, Version 65)

Asked for: compare the human figures with the paintings and list the differences.

How this was done: sixteen figures (ten people; eight in court dress, eight in war dress) were photographed whole from the front as the game draws them in Version 65 and set under the front figure of the artist's sheet for the same person and dress. "Paintings" is read here as the artist's sheets, which are the agreed true look. Only the front was compared; sides and backs were not. The other 69 models were not compared one by one, but they are built by the same rules, so the "everyone" list applies to them too. Where the story paintings differ from the sheets is a separate list in `Repaint_Brief.md`.

## Everyone

Biggest first.

1. **Shawl.** On the sheets it is a soft folded cloth laid over the left shoulder and hanging straight down to the knee, leaving the chest open. In the game it is a wide, flat, shiny band running corner to corner across the chest, like a pageant sash, and it hides the chest and the necklaces.
2. **Shawl colour.** The cross band is often not the sheet's colour: Arjuna gold (sheet blue), Bhima gold (red), Karna gold (green), Yudhishthira pink (cream), Duryodhana black (gold), Krishna red (yellow). The hanging end is the right colour.
3. **Lower cloth, court.** Sheets: a full dhoti in deep folds, ballooning at the thigh, gathered to each ankle, with a broad patterned border. Game: a straight, flat tube to mid shin, no folds, legs not separated.
4. **Lower cloth, war.** Sheets: tucked up to the knee in folds with a pointed front fall. Game: a straight short skirt, with a band of scales under the coat that the sheets do not have.
5. **Proportions.** Sheets: about seven and a half heads tall, long legs, very broad shoulders, narrow waist. Game: about six heads tall, big head, short legs, thicker waist. They read as dolls beside the sheets.
6. **Muscle.** Sheets show chest, stomach and arm muscle clearly. Game bodies are smooth.
7. **Necklaces.** Sheets: three to five strands with a large pendant. Game: one thin chain, mostly hidden by the shawl band.
8. **Waist.** Sheets: a broad wrapped waist cloth with a jewelled clasp and a tasselled front panel. Game: the front panel is there; the wrapped cloth and clasp are not.
9. **Crowns.** Sheets: tall, tiered, chased and jewelled, with ear pieces. Game: smooth plain domes with one gem. Earrings do not show.
10. **Faces.** Sheets: lifelike, each person different. Game: simple faces with large eyes, much alike.
11. **Skin.** Game skin is redder and darker than the sheets' warm tan (Krishna's blue and Satyaki's dark skin are right).
12. **Yellow cloth.** Where the sheet has strong golden yellow (Bhima, Karna, Krishna) the game has pale cream.
13. **Armour detail.** Sheets: each person has their own shoulder plates, arm guards and shin guards, with their sign repeated on them. Game: the same wing-shaped shoulder plate for all, plain tube arm guards and shin guards.
14. **Sandals.** Arjuna, Bhima, Duryodhana and Bhishma wear sandals in war on the sheets. Everyone is barefoot in the game.

## Person by person

Only what is not already covered above.

**Arjuna, court.** Crown wings much larger than the sheet's small diadem. The sheet's large gold pendant is missing.

**Arjuna, war.** The sheet has bare shoulders, no plates; the game adds plates. Sheet's chest sign is a wide fan of gold feathers; the game's is small. Leather arm and shin guards are right in colour but lack the gold studs and straps.

**Bhima, court.** Moustache right. Red waist cloth missing; build should be the broadest of all, and in the game he is barely wider than Karna.

**Bhima, war.** The lion's face on the chest reads as a blob. Shoulder plates should be large round domes with lions. No lion clasp at the waist, no lion knee pieces.

**Yudhishthira, court.** Slimmer build is right. Pink waist cloth has become the shawl band; the shawl itself should be cream.

**Karna, court.** Beard right. The green waist cloth with sun clasp is missing; green hanging end is right.

**Karna, war.** Chest sun is right. Suns on shoulders and shin guards missing.

**Duryodhana, court.** Goatee too small to read. The sheet's black and gold patterned waist cloth is missing.

**Duryodhana, war.** Sheet: black armour covered in gold scrollwork with blue stones. Game: plain glossy black with one stone. Lion shoulder plates missing.

**Draupadi.** Much too short and child-like beside the men; on the sheet she is nearly their height. Sheet: red blouse with bare waist, long loose hair to the hip, forehead jewel, pearl waist chain. Game: a small crown, hair hardly showing, the band covering the whole front.

**Krishna.** Peacock feather floats above the crown instead of sitting in it. Flower garland missing. Flute at the waist missing. Blue is darker than the sheet's.

**Kripa.** The closest match. Bead string is too short and thin; the white cloth lacks its folds.

**Dhrishtadyumna, war.** Tall spiked crown where the sheet has a low diadem and a hair knot. A gold cross band the sheet does not have. Red shoulder cloak missing. The flame pattern on the front panel is plain orange.

**Satyaki, war.** The game gives him a steel helmet; the sheet's front figure has a hair knot and gold diadem. The cross band should be orange, not gold. Orange border on the blue cloth missing.

**Bhishma, war.** Beard far too short: the sheet's reaches the chest. Bead string missing. Coat and white cloth right in colour.

**Drona, war.** Stands noticeably smaller and slighter than the others; on the sheet he is the same build. Coat is bronze-brown where the sheet has grey steel; arm guards are leather where the sheet has steel. Bead string and the red stripes on the cloth missing.

## What matches

Dress colours other than yellow, each person's hair and beard style, crown type, armlets, bracelets and anklets, coat metal (gold, silver, black), Karna's sun, Krishna's blue, Kripa's knot and ash lines.

## If only three things are done

1. Lay the shawl over the left shoulder as the sheets have it, in the sheet's colour, so the chest and necklaces show.
2. Lengthen the legs and shrink the head toward seven and a half heads.
3. Give the court dhoti its folds and separate legs.
