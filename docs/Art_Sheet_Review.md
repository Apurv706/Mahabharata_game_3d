# The artist's sheets against the brief (12 November)

181 pictures arrived. All 160 sheets the brief (`docs/brief/`) asked for are there, with 20 detail sheets (crowns, weapons, Karna's armour, Draupadi's jewellery) and one extra (Duryodhana grown, as a student). Every one was looked at.

## Meets the brief
- PNG, 3000 px or more, one look to a sheet, named `character_age_look`.
- Front, side and back at one height, arms clear of the body, head front and side, a cloth-winding diagram, swatches, a height scale, weapons drawn beside the figure.
- Every character, age and look in the three batches; four line-ups; horse, elephant and chariot (with parasol and standard).
- The signature features: Bhima the largest (195 cm), Sahadeva the slightest (178 cm), Ghatotkacha bald and 215 cm, Ekalavya's missing thumb, Shakuni's dice, Balarama's plough, Gandhari's blindfold, Karna's birth armour as child and youth.

## Differs from the brief
1. Karna at war still wears the sun ear-rings (the brief: after he has given armour and ear-rings away).
2. Draupadi's skin is the same mid-brown as Arjuna's (the brief: dark-skinned).
3. Draupadi in disguise has her hair in a plait (the brief: unbound from the dice hall until the war ends).
4. The war sheets show the same age as the court sheets (the brief: mid-forties, harder and more worn).

The models follow the brief on 1 to 3.

## Small gaps the brief allowed
- Most child and youth sheets have front and side only.
- No three-quarter head views; no layered source files.
- Whether the faces are original cannot be checked from the pictures.

## What the brief left out
- About 25 minor people the game shows: Bhurishravas, Kritavarma, Vrishasena, Iravan, Vikarna, Yudhamanyu, Uttamaujas; Bhadra, Madhavi, Revat, Sudatta, Haridatta; the player's mother, father, grandmother and cousin; rakshasa, naga, gandharva, yaksha. They wear the nearest model for now.
- Charu and Ugra as children and youths (taken down from their grown sheets).
- Each hero's own chariot banner.
- Any surroundings: palace, arena, village, camp.
